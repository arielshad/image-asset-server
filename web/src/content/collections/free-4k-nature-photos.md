## Landscapes at full size

These are nature photos with at least 2160 pixels on the short side: wide enough for a desktop wallpaper, a full-bleed hero image or an A4 print. Most come from Wikimedia Commons, where photographers upload originals straight from their cameras, and from Creative Commons photos that Openverse indexes from Flickr.

## Using them

Download the size you need rather than the original: a 1920 px copy is plenty for a website and loads far faster. Leave space for text where the picture has it (a calm sky, still water), and check the shape: a wide panorama crops badly to a phone screen.

Licences vary per photo. CC0 and public-domain images need nothing; CC BY needs the credit line shown in the detail view; CC BY-SA means an edited version must carry the same licence. Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay have more modern nature photography and are linked from every search.
