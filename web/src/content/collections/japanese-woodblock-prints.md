## Pictures of the floating world

Ukiyo-e, the woodblock prints of Edo-period Japan, show landscapes, actors, courtesans, travellers and waves in flat colour and bold line. Their designers (Hokusai, Hiroshige, Utamaro, Kuniyoshi) shaped Western art and design, and their prints have long been in the public domain.

These pages collect prints from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago and the Cleveland Museum of Art, all released under CC0, with more on Wikimedia Commons.

## Using them

Flat colour and strong outlines make these prints work as posters, book covers, backgrounds and patterns, and they survive heavy cropping. Download the original for print: many museum scans show the paper's texture, which looks good at large sizes.
