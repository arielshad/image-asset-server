## Surfaces for design

Texture adds warmth to flat design: aged paper behind a recipe, a wood grain under a product shot, linen behind a wedding invitation, concrete behind a tech headline. These pages collect photographed textures and backgrounds from Wikimedia Commons and Openverse.

## Using them

Lower the contrast or blend the texture with a colour so text stays readable. For repeating patterns, check whether the image tiles seamlessly, or mirror it at the edges. Download a size that matches the area: a small texture stretched across a 4K screen looks soft.

For 3D work, physically based material sets with normal and roughness maps are on the sister site 3d.shep.bot.
