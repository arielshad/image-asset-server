## Maps as pictures

Old maps are history, design and decoration at once: hand-coloured world maps with sea monsters, bird's-eye city views, nautical charts and railway maps. The Library of Congress has digitised hundreds of thousands, and Wikimedia Commons holds many more from libraries around the world.

## Using them

Crop details for backgrounds, book covers, game art and wall prints. Map scans are often enormous: download the original for print, a 1920 px copy for the web.

Check the rights statement on Library of Congress items: most old maps say *No known restrictions on publication*, shown here as free to use. A credit to the collection is a courtesy, and helps readers find the full map.
